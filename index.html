CHK—01

Suicide ideation & mental health crisis

Military Crisis Line: dial 988, press 1, or text 838255 — available 24/7.

⚠️ Suicidal ideation is NOT misconduct. Any associated misconduct must be considered separately and delicately. This requires immediate, compassionate, life-saving action. Never leave the Airman alone.

CHK—06

DUI or alcohol-related incident

Safety, notification, and follow-up after an alcohol-related incident.

Safety first: get everyone safe and off the road, then notify the chain of command. Do not question the Airman about the incident, and remind them they can speak with the Area Defense Counsel.
